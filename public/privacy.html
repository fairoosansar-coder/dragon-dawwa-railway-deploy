REVIEW INTERFACE PRIVACY

What this interface sends when you choose an action.

This notice describes the present review interface. It does not promise a future service, retention period, or provider feature that has not been configured and verified.

Choice matters.
No study history is selected by default.

Study questions

When you press Ask with sources, the question, optional ayah reference, selected response language and history choice are sent to the study endpoint. When the source-backed study feature is available, the server sends only the selected verse or text question to Quran.ai; it does not send an account ID. After your action, the question and retrieved evidence can be sent to Google for explanation or narration. The page does not publish a question or answer. Leaving the history box unchecked hides the answer from your saved history, but the private answer and retrieved-source record remain stored so its evidence and on-demand audio can still work. No automatic study-record expiry is active in this review; member-account deletion removes its linked answers, reports and private audio. Pseudonymous daily usage counters can remain after deletion until a separately approved cleanup policy is operated.

Audio and microphone

Narration is requested only after you select it; audio never starts automatically. The optional microphone control appears only in browsers that support recording and asks permission only after you choose Record. A recording is capped at 30 seconds and 2 MB before the transcription request. If transcription fails, the page asks you to type instead.

Reader and language choice

The interface language is stored in this browser as dd-language. The Qur’an reader states when reading content is retrieved through the application from Quran Foundation and when browser audio connects to a recitation delivery service. When you save or import a reader place, the server validates the selected chapter and verse with Quran Foundation. It does not send your Dragon Dawwa account identifier or private profile to that provider.

Do not put sensitive information in a study question

Use a question that can be safely processed for study. Do not include account credentials, payment information, precise address details, private messages, or information about another person. The configured provider account privacy tier has not been verified here, so do not assume a no-training or paid-tier protection. A source service or narration service can be unavailable; the interface shows an unavailable state rather than creating a substitute answer.

Account choices

Member-profile, saved-item and account-deletion information belongs to the private member experience. This review page does not claim that it can delete an upstream identity-provider account. Where an account option is unavailable, the interface should state that rather than imply completion.

Links to other services

Source and publisher links may open another website. Their privacy practices apply once you leave this interface. External links are labelled and use a separate browser destination.
