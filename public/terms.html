USE WITH CARE

Sources, explanations and community records are not the same thing.

Dragon Dawwa is a learning interface. It must not be used to manufacture attribution, present generated text as a source, or imply authority that has not been supplied.

Evidence before assertion.
Read the cited source.

Source material

Arabic Qur’an text, named translations, recitation recordings, government-directory records and external publisher resources remain subject to their respective sources and rights. A translation conveys an interpretation of meaning and is not the Arabic Qur’an itself.

Study assistance

A study response is generated assistance only when the server returns a structured response with displayed source references. It is not a published translation, a scholarly review, a personal fatwa, or a substitute for qualified human guidance. If sources cannot be retrieved, the interface should say so rather than invent support.

Community information

A directory listing is not proof of present contact details, activity, membership, ownership, programme approval, partnership or endorsement. Do not use a listed address to infer a mosque territory or identify worshippers. Confirm practical arrangements with an authorised local contact.

Respectful use

Do not submit material that attempts to override source rules, request secrets, target another person, or interfere with the service. Do not treat an unreviewed response as instruction to another person. For a question involving personal, legal, medical, safety or religious judgement, seek appropriately qualified local guidance.

External links and availability

External resources are links, not rehosted lessons. Their publishers control their own availability and terms. Review interfaces may show loading, unavailable or retry states; an unavailable integration is not evidence that a source says something else.

Changes and review status

This is a private review build. A control or route does not prove that a lesson, gathering, speaker, translation, narrator, mosque affiliation, or public release has been approved. Confirmed events and reviewed material are shown only when their actual records are available.
