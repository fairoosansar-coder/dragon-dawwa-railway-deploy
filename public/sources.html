SOURCES AND ATTRIBUTION

Read the source, not a claim about the source.

Dragon Dawwa’s public pages identify original publishers, government records, and the limits of each item. A link is not an endorsement or proof that a source supports every statement on this site.

Evidence before assertion.
Follow the named source.

Published readings

Library articles show their cited sources, contributors, available languages, and the editorial context supplied with the published revision. Editorial publication is not a scholarly endorsement, and a translation conveys an interpretation of meaning rather than replacing the Arabic Qur’an.

Directory and research records

Mosque directory pages preserve limited government-source fields and identify the original district document where available. A listing does not prove current contact details, activity, membership, ownership, programme approval, partnership, or endorsement.

Resources and events

Curated resources link to their original publishers and are shown only when their publication checks permit it. Events appear only when a published event record is available. An empty list is an honest empty state, not a promise of a future gathering or resource.

External services and availability

The Qur’an reader uses Quran Foundation content only when that integration is configured and available. Audio and external source links are controlled by their respective providers. When an integration is unavailable, Dragon Dawwa states that condition rather than generating substitute source material.

How to use this page

Open cited original sources, check dates and publication context, and seek qualified local guidance for personal religious, medical, legal, safety, or other professional decisions. Do not treat an unreviewed claim or an unavailable source as evidence.
