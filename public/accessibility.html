ACCESSIBILITY

Calm, direct controls that do not depend on motion.

Public pages include practical accessibility support, while avoiding claims of formal certification or perfect support in every browser and assistive technology.

Keyboard first.
Controls state what they do.

Keyboard and focus

Public pages include a Skip to content link, ordinary links and buttons, semantic labels where controls are present, visible focus styling, and live status regions for loading and error messages. Mobile navigation is a real button with an expanded state.

Language and script

The interface selector offers English, Tamil and Sinhala navigation. Qur’an Arabic is marked with Arabic language and right-to-left direction; it is not replaced by an automatic interface-language translation. Tamil and Sinhala fallback font families are included in the visitor styles.

Motion, reading and audio

Reduced-motion preferences remove nonessential animation and smooth scrolling. Qur’an audio does not autoplay. When a source, external link, browser audio feature, or configured reader integration is unavailable, the page provides an honest status or source-link alternative.

Helpful ways to use the site

Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move through links and controls; Enter or Space activates buttons. On a narrow screen, use the menu button to reveal navigation. Reader status messages are exposed with live regions.

Known boundaries

Native audio controls, external source sites, and browser capabilities are controlled by the visitor’s browser or the relevant external service. The current interface has responsive rules for narrow widths and reduced motion, but device- and assistive-technology-specific testing should be recorded separately rather than inferred from these controls.
