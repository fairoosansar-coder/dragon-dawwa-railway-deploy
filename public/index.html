TRUST BEGINS WITH TRANSPARENCY

Rooted in evidence.
Held with humility.

We aim to show where a teaching comes from, explain its context, and be open about what needs further study.

01Sources you can follow+

Qur’anic references link to the verse. Hadith references should identify the collection, wording, grading and scholar responsible for the assessment. A quotation and someone’s interpretation are clearly distinguished.

02Honesty about differences+

Scholars can differ about authenticity, interpretation and application. We aim to explain recognised disagreements fairly, without turning a difference of opinion into a judgement about someone’s faith.

03The willingness to correct+

A reliable community makes room for correction. Published teaching should carry its sources and review status, and a correction should appear wherever the original claim was shared.

04Dignity in every conversation+

We examine ideas with care and treat people with respect. Sincere questions are welcome. Humiliation, personal attacks and claims to know another person’s intentions have no place here.
