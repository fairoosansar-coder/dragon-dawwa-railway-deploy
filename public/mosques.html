WHAT THIS DIRECTORY DOES NOT ESTABLISH

A source is useful within its limits.

  • A government directory entry is displayed as a listed fact, not proof of a present affiliation, authority, ownership, approval, activity or contact channel.
  • Listed addresses are transcribed from the source and do not define a mosque territory or identify worshippers or members.
  • Spelling, place names and categories are kept close to the source. Report a transcription concern with evidence rather than treating this page as the final record.
