Topic & reading type

These optional labels help visitors find the reading. They describe the article’s editorial framing, not authenticity, qualification or scholarly endorsement. Quran and Hadith labels identify the cited material only; they are not authenticity grades or scholar endorsements.

Source references

Link to the original source and identify its author and passage. Use Quran and Hadith only to identify the cited material, not to assert authenticity. Distinguish source text, translation, interpretation and editorial context. References are required before review.

Human review & publication

Read every included language and reference before approving. Record actual contributor credits, context, uncertainties and corrections. Approval is an editorial decision, not a claim of scholarly endorsement. The owner may self-review, which is not independent review.
