Recover your private sign-in

This invite-only version uses Tailscale Serve to verify the identity of each connected member. Dragon Dawwa does not hold or reset a Tailscale, Google, Microsoft, Apple, GitHub or other provider password. Tailscale’s identity guide explains the supported sign-in methods.

If your device is disconnected, sign into the Tailscale app with the same identity provider and account your invitation used, and ask the owner to confirm that your exact login remains invited. Recover a lost password or passkey directly with that identity provider. On a shared device, sign out of Tailscale as well as Dragon Dawwa when finished.

Return to Dragon Dawwa. A restored provider login does not grant private access until the owner explicitly invites that verified account. Never send passwords, recovery codes or identity documents to Dragon Dawwa.
